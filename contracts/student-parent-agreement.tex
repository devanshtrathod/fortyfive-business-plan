\section{What the words mean}

\begin{clauses}
  \item \textbf{Platform} means the FortyFive website and apps, including lessons, messages, bookings and payments.
  \item \textbf{Tutor} means a person approved by FortyFive to teach on the Platform. A \textbf{Coach} is a Tutor, aged 18 or over, approved to give coursework coaching.
  \item \textbf{Lesson} means a tutoring session booked and held on the Platform. A \textbf{Coaching Session} means an hour of coursework coaching. A \textbf{Course} means a recorded course sold on the Platform. The \textbf{Marker} means our coursework check, which gives a report of bands, evidence and questions.
  \item \textbf{Price} means the full amount shown on a booking or a Course, including GST.
  \item \textbf{Safeguarding Lead} means the adult FortyFive names as responsible for safety reports, currently \ph{name}, reachable at \ph{safety email} and \ph{phone}.
  \item \textbf{Under-18} means a Student who is under 18 years old.
\end{clauses}

\section{What FortyFive is, and is not}

\begin{clauses}
  \item FortyFive is a place to find IB Diploma Courses, Tutors and coursework coaching, with free tools. We check every Tutor, take payments, keep the records and handle reports.
  \item Tutors are independent professionals, not our employees. They set their own prices and hours, and they are responsible for their own teaching. We are responsible for how we check them and how we run the Platform.
  \item Nobody can promise an IB grade. FortyFive and its Tutors will never promise or guarantee a grade, a predicted grade or a university place.
  \item FortyFive is not affiliated with or endorsed by the International Baccalaureate Organization. We never use or share IB past papers or markschemes.
\end{clauses}

\section{Accounts and a Parent's approval}

\begin{clauses}
  \item An Under-18's account needs a Parent's approval before the Student can message a Tutor, book a Lesson, post a request or use the Marker. Until then the Student can browse, use the free tools, watch Course previews and save Tutors.
  \item To approve, the Parent confirms their identity and their relationship to the Student \ph{through DigiLocker or another method our lawyer confirms}, and gives the consent in Schedule 2. This is the verifiable consent the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules require before we process a child's personal data.
  \item Each person has one account. A Student can link up to two Parents. Keep your password to yourself, and tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{What a Parent can see and do}

\begin{clauses}
  \item A Parent of an Under-18 can see every booking, read every message between their child and a Tutor (read-only), read every help log and Marker report, and see every payment.
  \item A Parent can join any Lesson or Coaching Session as an observer, and can message a Tutor in their own thread.
  \item A Parent approves and pays for every booking and every Course. An Under-18 never pays.
  \item A Parent may ask the Safeguarding Lead to look at a recording that is part of a concern (clause 8.3).
  \item \textbf{At 18.} On the Student's 18th birthday we ask the Student whether their Parent should keep seeing their account. Until the Student answers, nothing changes. After that, the Student decides, and the Student becomes the party to this agreement for anything booked from then on.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{Booking}

\begin{clauses}
  \item The Student chooses a Tutor and a time and sends a request. For an Under-18, the request goes to the Parent, who approves and pays. We hold the slot for 12 hours while the Parent decides.
  \item The Tutor accepts or declines within 24 hours. If they do not, the request lapses and any payment is refunded in full.
  \item Lessons and Coaching Sessions for Under-18s take place only between 7 am and 9 pm in the Student's local time, only online, and only on the Platform. The Platform does not offer slots outside these hours.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{Prices and payment}

\begin{clauses}
  \item The Price shown is the full price, including GST. There are no booking fees or extras on top. Every booking shows a receipt of where the money goes: the Tutor keeps 90\% of a Lesson and a Coach keeps 85\% of a Coaching Session, and the rest is FortyFive's fee.
  \item Courses cost Rs 1,499 including GST. Anyone who asks can have a free place on any Course, with no forms, no proof and no questions. A Marker check costs Rs 499, and is also free if you ask.
  \item Payments go through our payment provider, \ph{name}. We never see or store full card details. We send a GST invoice for every payment.
\end{clauses}

\section{Recordings}

\begin{clauses}
  \item When the Parent sets up the account, they choose whether Lessons are recorded (Schedule 2). Coaching Sessions are always recorded, so the help given can be checked.
  \item Recordings are kept for \ph{90 days}, or longer if a report or review needs them. They can be opened only by the Safeguarding Lead and our integrity reviewers, and every opening is logged. We never use recordings for marketing or to train AI.
  \item A Parent may ask the Safeguarding Lead to review a recording that is part of a concern. Students, Parents and Tutors cannot download recordings.
  \item Nobody, including the Student and the Parent, may record, screenshot or copy a Lesson, a Coaching Session or a message thread.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{Rules for Students}

\begin{clauses}
  \item Be respectful to Tutors and to FortyFive staff.
  \item Keep everything on the Platform. Never give a Tutor your phone number, email, social media handle or address, and never ask for theirs.
  \item Tell your Parent, or use the Report button, if a Tutor ever:
  \begin{clauses}
    \item asks to talk, meet or pay anywhere other than FortyFive;
    \item asks you to keep a secret, or to leave your Parent out;
    \item offers gifts, or talks about personal, romantic or sexual things; or
    \item does or says anything that makes you uncomfortable.
  \end{clauses}
  You will never be in trouble for reporting something honestly.
  \item Leave honest reviews, only about the teaching.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{Coursework}

\begin{clauses}
  \item Coursework help on FortyFive follows the IB's rules. Coaches teach the skills the criteria reward and ask questions. They never write, rewrite, edit, translate, proofread, correct or mark up the work, and no files change hands.
  \item Coaching is capped per piece of work: Internal Assessment 4 hours, Extended Essay 6 hours, TOK essay 3 hours, TOK exhibition 2 hours.
  \item After every Coaching Session the Coach files a help log, and the Student adds their own next step. The Student should tell their supervisor that they are getting help and share their help logs.
  \item Marker reports give bands, evidence from the work and questions to think about. They never rewrite the work and never predict a final IB grade. Files sent to the Marker are deleted 48 hours after the report is ready.
  \item The Student signs the Coursework Code in Schedule 1. If it is broken, we may stop coaching and Marker checks for that piece of work.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{Safety and reports}

\begin{clauses}
  \item There is a Report button on every screen, and a report form on our website that needs no account. The Safeguarding Lead reads every report within 24 hours, and the same day if it is about a child's safety.
  \item If a child is in immediate danger, call 112. Childline is 1098.
  \item Where the law requires, including under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, we report concerns to the police without first holding our own inquiry. We may share information with the police and other authorities as the law allows.
  \item We may suspend a Tutor at once while we look into a concern, and refund any bookings with them.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{Personal data}

\begin{clauses}
  \item We keep only what we need: names, school, subjects, exam session, bookings, messages, help logs, payments, consent records and reports. Our Privacy Notice explains each one and how long we keep it.
  \item Tutors see only a Student's first name, DP year, subjects and exam session. They never see the Student's school, surname or contact details.
  \item We never show ads, never sell or share data for marketing, and never track how students behave or target them with advertising, as section 9 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 requires for children.
  \item You can download your data, correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to \ph{privacy email}. Some records (payments, consent records and safety reports) must be kept for the time the law requires.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{Reviews}

\begin{clauses}
  \item Only a Student or Parent who paid for a finished Lesson or Coaching Session can review it. Tutors may reply once. We remove a review only if it is abusive, reveals personal data, or is not about the teaching.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{What we are responsible for}

\begin{clauses}
  \item We check every Tutor carefully, but we cannot guarantee how any person will behave, and we are not responsible for exam results.
  \item Except where the law does not allow it, our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 3 months before the claim. Nothing in this agreement takes away your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================
\section{Closing an account}

\begin{clauses}
  \item You may close the account at any time. Bookings not yet held are cancelled and refunded in full.
  \item We may suspend or close an account that breaks this agreement. We will tell you why, and give you a chance to respond, unless a safety concern means we must act at once.
\end{clauses}

% =====================================================================

\begin{clauses}
  \item We may change this agreement or our Policies to keep children safer, to follow the law or to improve the Platform. We will give at least 30 days' notice of a change that affects you, unless a change is needed sooner for safety or by law. If you do not accept it, you may close the account before it takes effect.
\end{clauses}
