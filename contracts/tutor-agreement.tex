\schedule{3}{Coursework coaching}

The IB treats help from tutors that goes beyond what the subject guide allows as a problem for the Student, and work written even partly by someone else (or by AI) is not the Student's own. These rules exist so that our coaching can never cost a Student their Diploma.

\subsection*{You may}
\begin{itemize}
  \item explain what each assessment criterion asks for, and what separates the levels;
  \item teach the methods: research, choosing and judging sources, collecting and analysing data, structuring an argument, referencing;
  \item help a Student find and narrow their own topic and research question, by asking questions;
  \item talk through their ideas and plans, and ask questions that make them think harder;
  \item use your own examples, or published examples the Student's school allows, to show a skill.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{You must never}
\begin{itemize}
  \item write, rewrite, edit, proofread, translate or polish any part of the work;
  \item take, open, mark up or annotate the Student's file, or have it shared on screen for you to correct;
  \item predict a grade or mark for the work;
  \item give the Student a research question, thesis, data set, analysis or sources they have not found themselves;
  \item help with any work the Student's school says must be done without help.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Help logs}

Within 24 hours of every Coaching Session you file a help log on the Platform: what was covered, what questions you asked, and the next step the Student chose for themselves. The Student adds their own note and can show the log to their supervisor. Your payout for the session waits for the log.

\subsection*{Integrity reviews}

An IB teacher working for FortyFive reviews the recording of 1 in every 10 Coaching Sessions, chosen at random, and any session that is flagged. If a review finds help beyond these rules, we may refund the session, end this agreement under clause 14.3, and tell the Parent.
