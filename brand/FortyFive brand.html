FortyFive brand guide

The permanent logo: the full stop. “45.” in bold numerals on an ink square, beside the wordmark in Latin Modern. Forty-five, full stop: nothing else. Everything is outlined from Latin Modern (GUST Font License), so the files never depend on a font.

The two other directions explored are in LOGO OPTIONS.html. The name stays FortyFive; the recommended domain is joinfortyfive.com (not bought yet).

The logo

Primary lockup, on paper or white
Reversed, on ink
Stacked, for square spaces
The monogram: app icon, favicon, avatar

Space and size

Keep clear space of at least half the tile's height on every side.
Smallest: the lockup 20 px tall, the monogram 16 px.

Colour

Ink #121211Text, the logo, main buttons
Paper #f6f4eeBackgrounds. Never pure white.
Blue #1f63b8Links, verified ticks, one accent only

Red #b42a2a is only for Report and safety notices. Green #1f7a3a is only for a done tick.

Don't

×
×
×IB FortyFive
  • Don't stretch, squash, rotate or outline the logo.
  • Use only ink or paper for it. No gradients, no shadows, no glow.
  • Never put “IB” inside the name or the logo, and always use the disclaimer: “Not affiliated with or endorsed by the International Baccalaureate Organization.”
  • Don't retype the wordmark in another font. Use the files.
